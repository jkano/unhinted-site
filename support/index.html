Write to unhinted@jdkno.com. Tell us what happened, which phone and iOS version you have, and the room code if there was one. We read everything.

How do I play?

Open the app and tap How to play on Home for a short practice round. In short: everyone except the Seeker writes one word that points to a secret word. Words that match cancel each other out. The Seeker guesses from what is left, and everybody scores together.

How do I join a friend's room?

Type the six-character code on Home, tap Paste if you copied it, or tap Scan QR and point the camera at the code on the host's phone or the TV.

How do I show the game on a TV?

In a room, tap Show on TV. The app does not pick the screen for you: it shows three steps to follow.

  1. Open Control Center and tap Screen Mirroring.
  2. Pick your Apple TV or AirPlay screen. The TV shows only the Board.
  3. Keep playing on your phone. The TV never shows the word.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, tap your avatar on Home, scroll down and tap Delete my data (guest) or Delete account (signed in with email). Everything is erased and the app starts over. More in the privacy policy.

Someone is spoiling the game. What can I do?

The host can remove or ban a player from the room's player list. Tell us about anyone who breaks the rules and we will act.
